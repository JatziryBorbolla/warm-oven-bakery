Story of the Bakery

Warm Oven Bakery started as a small neighborhood kitchen dedicated to the art of traditional baking. Built on early mornings and simple, honest ingredients, our goal has always been to bring people together over freshly baked sourdough, hand-laminated pastries, and handcrafted treats. Every loaf and cake is made from scratch with genuine care for our local community.

Behind the Scenes at Our Bakery

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that great baking starts with exceptional ingredients. We partner with local farmers and regional millers to ensure everything coming out of our ovens is wholesome, fresh, and sustainable.

  • Stone-Ground Flours: 100% unbleached flours milled from regional grain producers.
  • Farm-Fresh Dairy: European-style butter, fresh cream, and milk sourced from local dairy farms.
  • Seasonal Produce: Locally harvested fruit, berries, and natural sweeteners used at peak ripeness.
